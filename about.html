About

My name is Cherisha Killari Giribabu, and I created Explore Atlanta as a simple guide to a city that has become an important part of my life. I moved to Atlanta to attend Georgia State University for my master's degree, so I wanted my website to focus on a place I am currently getting to know.

I chose this topic because Atlanta has many different sides to it. The city has well-known attractions, outdoor spaces, history, and food from many cultures. Building this website gave me a chance to share a few of the places and experiences that stood out to me while also practicing HTML and CSS.

My motivation was to create something useful for another student or visitor who may be new to the city. I wanted the pages to be easy to read and navigate, with information that helps someone decide what to see or eat. As I explore more of Atlanta, I can continue adding new places and recommendations to the website.
